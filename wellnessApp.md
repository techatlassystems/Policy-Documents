# HerCycle: Period & Fertility & Wellness App Privacy Policy

# ⚠️ Predictions, Pregnancy Planning, and Not Medical Advice Disclaimer

**HerCycle: Period & Fertility App** is strictly an informational and lifestyle tracking tool.

* **Mathematical Estimates Only:** Any predictions or calculations displayed by the app (including but not limited to predicted period dates, fertile windows, conception windows, and ovulation estimates) are statistical approximations based on historical data entered by the user. They are not absolute guarantees and should not be treated as clinically accurate metrics.
* **Pregnancy Planning Disclaimer:** The pregnancy planning, fertility window estimation, and cycle tracking features are designed solely for educational and informational support. This app is **not** an infertility treatment, medical fertility monitor, or diagnostic device. It cannot guarantee pregnancy or predict the exact moment of conception.
* **No Medical Services:** The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of any health condition (including reproductive or fertility disorders).
* **Not for Contraception:** The app should not be relied upon as a method of contraception or birth control to avoid pregnancy.
* **Consult a Professional:** Menstrual cycles and fertility windows vary wildly based on stress, age, illness, nutrition, and personal biology. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare professional or fertility specialist with any questions regarding reproductive health or conception. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of predictions or estimates shown in this app.
